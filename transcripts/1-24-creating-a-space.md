# Creating a Space

BEMA 24 · Session 1

## Speakers

- Marty Solomon
- Brent Billings
- Josh Bossé

## Book of Bible

Exodus (the Tabernacle)

This is a practical episode on spiritual practices and does not work through a specific scripture passage. The conversation reflects on the Tabernacle story as a whole — the idea that where God once created a space and said "you fill it" (Genesis 1-3), God now says "you create the space, and I will fill it." Passing references include Genesis 1-3, the woman at the well and worshiping "in spirit and in truth" (John 4), and the Shema (Deuteronomy 6).

### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Mishkan** (מִשְׁכָּן) — the Tabernacle, the dwelling place; the mobile space God's people built and God filled with his presence.
- **ronan / rinnah** (רִנָּה) — a joyful shout; the first such word in the Hebrew Bible, the root behind "falling on joyful faces" (previous episode) and Josh's son's name.
- **Shema** (שְׁמַע) — "Hear, O Israel..." prayed morning and night as a text-centered prayer practice.
- **Baruch atah Adonai Eloheinu melech ha'olam** (בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם) — "Blessed are you, LORD our God, King of the universe," the opening of a Jewish blessing; tradition encourages up to 70 blessings a day as a practice of gratitude.
- **Olam** (עוֹלָם) — "world / eternity"; the name of Josh's communal spiritual-practice ministry.
