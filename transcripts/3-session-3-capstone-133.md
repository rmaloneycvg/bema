## Book of Bible

Review — Genesis through the Gospels

*This capstone episode is a full narrative review from Genesis through the Gospels; no single passage is read. Key reference points touched along the arc:*

- **Genesis 1–11** — the Preface (the story is good; trust it).
- **Genesis 12–50** — the Introduction (the family of God: Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph).
- **Exodus–Deuteronomy** — the Narrative begins, "A Tale of Two Kingdoms" (Empire vs. Shalom): rescue, marriage at Sinai, the tabernacle, Leviticus (a kingdom of priests), Numbers (desert honeymoon), Deuteronomy (remember — care for the alien, orphan, widow).
- **Joshua / Judges / Ruth** — the crossroads of the earth; the redemption cycle; righteous Ruth and Boaz.
- **Samuel–Kings (Israel) & Chronicles (Judah)** — the monarchy; Saul, David, Solomon's lust for empire; the divided kingdom.
- **Wisdom** — Psalms (songs), Proverbs (generally-true sayings), Ecclesiastes (meaning), Song of Songs (intimacy/dowd).
- **Prophets** — pre-Assyrian (Amos, Hosea; Micah, 1 Isaiah), Assyrian (Jonah, Nahum; Zephaniah, 2 Isaiah), Babylonian (Jeremiah, Lamentations, Habakkuk, Obadiah, Joel), exilic (Ezekiel, Daniel, Job, 3 Isaiah), remnant (Esther, Ezra–Nehemiah, Haggai, Zechariah, 4 Isaiah, Malachi).
- **The Silent Years** — synagogue & the Text / education; Hellenism and the five responses (Sadducees, Herodians, Essenes, Pharisees, Zealots).
- **The Gospels** — Matthew (the *mamzer*; Jewish author → Jewish audience), Mark (Roman; Jewish author → Roman audience), Luke ("order"/parashah companion; proselyte author), John ("grafted"/blended Greek–Jewish family).

### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Empire vs. Shalom** — the two kingdoms the whole Bible contrasts.
- **mamzer / mumzer** — the outsider; Matthew's agenda.
- **AOW** — the alien, the orphan, and the widow (Deuteronomy's charge).
- **dowd** — intimate/erotic love (Song of Songs).
- **t'shuva** — repentance / return (Zephaniah's message).
- **chutzpah** (hutzpah) — bold fire (Nehemiah's passionate leadership).
- **discipleship** — Marty's clarification: rabbinic "come, follow me" 24/7 discipleship (for a select few) is distinct from, and not a replacement for, evangelism ("making converts"), church assimilation, and spiritual formation — all of which should continue.
- **the Eastern view of truth** — truth unfolds and is revealed progressively (hence the capstones keep getting longer).
- Coming in Session 4: **Acts as the Epilogue** to the narrative, then the New Testament letters, then (Session 5) church history.
