### Hebrew Words Explained

- **mamzer** (also **mumzer**) — illegitimate child / outcast; Matthew's recurring agenda for the outsider.
- **talmid** (pl. **talmidim**) — disciple(s); those who follow and imitate the rabbi. The Talmud holds the rabbi–disciple bond as stronger even than parent–child, which frames Jesus's "loves father or mother more than me" saying.
- **Son of Man** — the Daniel 7 figure; here tied back to the paralytic story ("the Son of Man has authority to forgive sins"), suggesting the "coming" the disciples will see is about forgiveness.
- **ayin tovah / ayin ra'ah** — a "good eye" (generosity) vs. a "bad eye" (scarcity); invoked again regarding the kind of reward one reaps.
- **the person of peace (shalom)** — the "worthy person" the disciples are to stay with in each town.
- **the five discourses** — Matthew's five teaching blocks, paralleling the five books of Moses (presenting Jesus as the new Moses).
