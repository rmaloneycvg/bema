### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Essenes** — the separatist (largely priestly) group who, revolted by the temple's corruption, withdrew to the desert (e.g. Qumran) to "know the path and walk the path" — to copy and keep the text and prepare for God's coming judgment, seeing themselves as the "sons of light."
- **Qumran** — the desert compound where the Dead Sea Scrolls were found and copied.
- **mikvah** (מִקְוֶה) — the immersion pool; the Essenes required theirs to be filled with living water, so they built a dam and plastered canal to bring rainwater down the wadi.
- **maim chaim** (מַיִם חַיִּים) — "living water": water that comes directly from God (spring or rain, carried only by gravity), never stored by human hands.
- **Nazarite** (נָזִיר) — one under a vow of dedication to the LORD; John the Baptist shows signs of a Nazarite vow and Essene influence.

## Other Book References and Recommendations

- Jeremiah 6:16 ("stand at the crossroads... ask where the good way is, and walk in it") — a defining Essene text
- Isaiah 40:1-5 ("Comfort, comfort my people... a voice of one crying in the wilderness, prepare the way of the LORD") — the Essene (and John the Baptist) call
- The Dead Sea Scrolls and the four-person scribal copying method (reciter, checker, writer, checker; washing in a mikvah at every divine name) — why our Bible text is ~98% reliable across a millennium
- John the Baptist baptizing at the Jordan less than three miles from Qumran; his father Zachariah's likely Essene connections
- Ray Vander Laan: if God finds a people as devoted to knowing and walking the path, "the glory of the LORD will be revealed" again — part of the impulse behind BEMA
