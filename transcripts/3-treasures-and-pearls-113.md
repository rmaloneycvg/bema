### Hebrew Words Explained

- **PaRDeS** — p'shat (surface), remez (hint to a Hebrew text), drash (derived truth), sod (God-given mystery).
- **pearl / jewel** — in Hebrew idiom a "pearl" is a proverb/teaching/nugget of wisdom; biblical Hebrew has few words for gems, so "jewel" and "pearl" are effectively interchangeable.
- **the man / the merchant = God** — across all of Matthew 13's parables the main actor is God (sower, field owner, farmer, the woman baking). So the one who finds and buys the treasure/pearl is God finding *his people*, not primarily us finding wisdom.
- Ezekiel 16's "entered puberty" — the Hebrew is literally "came to excellent ornaments / full adornment" (a beautiful jewel), part of Ezekiel's deliberately provocative, sexualized imagery that English translations tend to soften.
- **three human images of God in the Psalms** — father, nursing mother, and shepherd; echoed in Jesus's Luke 15 trio (lost sheep / lost coin / lost son).
