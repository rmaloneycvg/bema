### Hebrew Words Explained

- **mamzer** — illegitimate child; the "who's in and who's out" theme running through Matthew.
- **s'micha** — authority; the authority a rabbi has to reinterpret Torah (why the crowds were amazed).
- **orthodoxy** — "right belief" (*ortho* = right, *doxy* = belief).
- **orthopraxy** — "right practice" (*ortho* = right, *praxy* = practice); the "fruit" by which teachers are known.
- **wadi** — a dry desert streambed that floods; the "sand" is the sediment at the bottom of a wadi, versus building on bedrock (the cliff face).
