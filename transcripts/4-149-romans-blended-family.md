### Hebrew Words Explained

- **theosabes / theosebeis** (“God-fearers”) — the Gentile believers in Rome; the church contains *new* theosabes (fresh out of paganism), *mature* theosabes (long transformed), and Jews (the original core, including proselytes). Romans addresses this blended family, not an abstract treatise on salvation.
- **miqsat ma'ase haTorah / “works of the law”** — “no one will be declared righteous … by the works of the law” (Rom 3:20). Paul's point is that *every* group's measuring stick fails them, so justification cannot come from works.
- **justification vs. righteousness from God** — Paul announces “a righteousness that is by faith from first to last” (Rom 1:17, quoting Habakkuk's “the righteous will live by faith”). To be *justified* / *declared righteous* is the issue; the righteousness comes from God through faith, not from our obedience.
- **remez** (Hebrew, “hint”) — Paul's buried-allusion technique. “Every human being a liar … proved right when you judge” is a *remez* to **Psalm 51** (David confronted over Bathsheba) — i.e., “we Jews know what sin is, it's in our own story.”
- **“stringing pearls”** (Hebrew *charaz*) — the rabbinic art of chaining many scripture quotations together. Romans 3:10-18 strings six-plus *remez-im* (Psalms 14/53, Ecclesiastes 7, Psalm 5, Psalm 140, Psalm 10 LXX, Isaiah 59, Psalm 36). On the surface it's one indictment; underneath, a Jewish sermon only the Torah-keepers would fully catch — and every quoted text is about *Israel*, so Paul is saying “don't point at the Gentiles; sin is in our own songs.”
- **circumcision of the heart** — “a person is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is circumcision of the heart by the Spirit” (Rom 2:29).
- **sarx / Hellenism** — Paul's opening indictment (Rom 1:18-32) is an *exact definition of pagan Hellenism*: “man is the measure of all things” (Pythagoras/Protagoras), exchanging God-as-measuring-rod for the self and created images.
