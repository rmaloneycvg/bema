### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Jacob → Israel / Yisrael** (ישראל) — "he struggles/fights with God"; the name given at the Jabbok wrestling, becoming the name of God's whole people (and repeated/confirmed in Genesis 35).
- **Peniel / Penuel** (פניאל) — "face of God"; "I have seen God face to face and survived." Jacob later tells Esau "to see your face is like seeing the face of God" — supporting the Midrash that he wrestled the "spirit of Esau."
- **Ben-Oni** (בן אוני) — "son of my misery/sorrow"; Rachel's dying name for her son.
- **Benjamin / Ben-Yamin** (בנימין) — "son of my right hand / son of favor"; the name Jacob gives instead.
- **"to my sorrow" / "my fault"** — the Hebrew phrase Jacob later uses of Rachel's death can carry the sense "it is because of me" — read (via Ray Vander Laan) as Jacob taking blame, recalling his rash curse on whoever stole Laban's idols (which Rachel had hidden).
