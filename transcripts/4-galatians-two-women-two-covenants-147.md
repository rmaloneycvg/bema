### Hebrew Words Explained

- **b'nei Avraham** (“children/sons of Abraham”) — full covenant members of Abraham's family. Paul's revolutionary claim: a God-fearing Gentile *with faith* is adopted as a true child of Abraham, not merely a “son of Noah” — a step beyond even Hillel.
- **theosabes / theosebeis** (“God-fearers”) — the Gentile audience Paul repeatedly calls “brothers” and “children of the promise,” insisting they already belong by faith.
- **miqsat ma'ase haTorah** (“some of the works of the law”) — the identity-marker works (circumcision, kosher, etc.); Paul argues the Gentiles need not live under these because they are justified by faith.
- **Ben Noach / b'nei Noach** (Hebrew, “son(s) of Noah”) — how a Gentile under the universal Noahic covenant may identify at a synagogue (“I am Ben Noach”). Welcomed — whereas claiming to be a *son of Abraham* without conversion would scandalize an Orthodox Jewish community.
- **The two women / two covenants allegory** — Paul's rabbinic *allegory* (old NIV: “interpreted allegorically”): **Hagar** = the Sinai covenant and the “present Jerusalem,” bearing children into slavery; **Sarah** (notably unnamed in the text) = the covenant of promise, the “Jerusalem above,” the free woman. The circumcision party, committed to law, maps onto *Hagar*, not Sarah — the opposite of what they would want.
- **Shammai exclusivity** — the strict view that Gentiles must convert (step under the Sinai covenant via circumcision) to belong; Paul calls exclusivity the “anti-Gospel.”
- **euangelion / gospel** — the announcement of a new King and a new Kingdom; its heart is radical inclusion by faith, “preached beforehand to Abraham.”
- The four covenants diagrammed: **Noahic** (God + all creation), **Abrahamic** (God + Abraham's descendants — Isaac, Ishmael, Keturah's line, Jacob; by faith, not circumcision), **Sinai/Mosaic** (God + the descendants of Jacob who stood at Sinai), and the adoption of faithful Gentiles into the *Abrahamic* family.

## Other Book References and Recommendations

- *The Epic of Eden* by **Sandra Richter** — recommended for its treatment of biblical covenants.
- **Genesis 15–17** — Paul's running proof text: Abraham justified by faith (15:6) *before* his circumcision (17); the promise precedes the law given “430 years later.”
- The **Sarah/Hagar** narrative (Genesis 16, 21) — the source story Paul turns into his allegory; “get rid of the slave woman and her son” (Gen 21:10).
- **Isaiah 54:1** — “Be glad, barren woman …” quoted in Gal 4:27 for the “desolate woman” with more children.
- **Hillel vs. Shammai** — Paul goes beyond even Hillel in granting Gentiles full Abrahamic sonship by faith.
- Note that a Torah-observant Jew does **not** cease to be a Jew under Paul's gospel — the Sinai covenant remains an everlasting calling; Paul's gospel simply adopts Gentiles into the family with their own calling.
