## Book of Bible

Torah (Genesis–Deuteronomy)

This is a capstone review episode summarizing the five books of Moses; it does not work through a specific scripture passage. The hosts trace the narrative arc of Torah and condense it to a single theme — **partnership**:

- **Genesis** — the *preface* (ch. 1-11: the goodness of creation and the invitation to trust) and *introduction* (ch. 12-50: the family of God — Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph). The **basis** of the partnership.
- **Exodus** — rescue from Egypt and the wedding at Sinai; God **chooses** his partner and the partner chooses God. Ends with the Tabernacle.
- **Leviticus** — the owner's manual of priesthood; where the partnership is **defined**.
- **Numbers** — the desert honeymoon; where God **shapes** the partner.
- **Deuteronomy** — the call to **remember** the story of the partnership.

- **A-I-J-J** — the whiteboard shorthand for the family of God in the Introduction: Avram, Yitzhak, Ya'akov, Yosef (Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph).
- **A-O-W** — shorthand for the Alien (foreigner), Orphan, and Widow — the groups Deuteronomy calls Israel to remember and pull in.
- **Tanakh** (תַּנַ"ךְ) — the Hebrew Bible; Session 1 covered the Torah, Session 2 will cover the rest of the Tanakh (Joshua through Malachi).
