### Hebrew Words Explained

- **kadosh** (קָדוֹשׁ) — holy; to be set apart, distinct, different. God asks the priests to be set apart from Israel, and Israel to be set apart from the nations — not because other ways are wrong, but to put God on display.
- **moed / moadim** (מוֹעֵד / מוֹעֲדִים) — appointed time(s), season(s), festival(s). The word at the center of the Genesis 1 chiasm; in Leviticus it names the biblical feasts — the God-ordained parties.
- **kashrut / kosher** (כַּשְׁרוּת / כָּשֵׁר) — the dietary and purity laws (Leviticus 11-20); their purpose is distinctness (a peculiar people), not merely ancient health benefit.
- **tallit** (טַלִּית) — the prayer garment with tassels on its four corners; each tassel carries a blue thread (Numbers 15) — blue being the color of priesthood — a reminder of the call to be a kingdom of priests.
