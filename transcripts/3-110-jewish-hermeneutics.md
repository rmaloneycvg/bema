### Hebrew Words Explained

- **PaRDeS** — the four levels of Jewish interpretation (an acronym): **p'shat**, **remez**, **drash**, **sod**.
- **p'shat** — the surface/plain reading. Not shallow in value: most Christian exegesis and expository preaching lives here, and it can be profound. The deeper levels never contradict it.
- **remez** — a "hint"; a word/phrase deliberately buried in the teaching that links to a Hebrew Scripture, unlocking deeper meaning (requires knowing the Text). E.g., "a hundredfold" points to Genesis 26:12.
- **drash** — truth hidden in story; the deeper meaning the remez unlocks. Meant to be discussed and debated in a group (havurah), not solved alone.
- **sod** — mystery; the deepest level. Cannot be taught or learned — it is given by God / the Holy Spirit (e.g., Peter's confession in Matthew 16:17).
- **havurah** — a learning fellowship; the group within which drash is wrestled out.
- **discovery** — the engine of Eastern teaching: a rabbi *buries* truth so students experience/discover it; parables make learning harder on purpose.
- **chutzpah** — bold fire; the Kingdom is for those who want it (Jacob over Esau).
