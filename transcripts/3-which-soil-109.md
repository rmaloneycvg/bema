### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Hayamim** — "the days"; the name of Marty's prototype weekend event.
- **the four learners** — a common rabbinic teaching template Jesus follows: the **sponge** (keeps everything), the **funnel** (loses everything), the **strainer** (keeps the bad), the **sieve** (keeps the good). The point is always "which are you?"
- **the Parable of the Soils** (not the Sower) — the sower is God; the variable is the soil, so the question is which soil *you* are, not whom you sow to.
- **tzedakah** — righteousness / generosity; Hosea 10:12's "break up your unplowed ground" is how the hard path prepares its soil.
- **hagah** — to meditate/mutter on the Text day and night; how the rocky soil grows deep roots (the acacia tree by living water).
- **ayin ra'ah** — a "bad eye" (scarcity), referenced regarding the strainer who keeps the bad.
- **p'shat / remez / drash** — levels of interpretation (to be explored next episode).
