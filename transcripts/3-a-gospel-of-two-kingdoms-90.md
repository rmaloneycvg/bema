### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Empire vs. Shalom** — the recurring "tale of two kingdoms" from Sessions 1-2, now framing the birth narratives: Herod's kingdom of self-preservation, fear, power, and wealth versus God's kingdom of self-sacrifice, invitation, and peace.
- **"Out of Egypt I called my son"** (Matthew 2:15, quoting Hosea 11:1) — Matthew casts Jesus as a new Israel reliving the nation's story; Joseph the dreamer who goes down to Egypt echoes the first Joseph.
- **Herodium** — one of Herod's three escape palaces, built on a mountain he had constructed; Jesus is born almost literally "in the shadow of the Herodium."
- **Masada** (מְצָדָה) — "fortress"; Herod's desert palace-fortress with the world's largest cisterns.
- **Caesar's Comet** — the star Octavian claimed was Julius Caesar ascending to godhood, making Octavian (Augustus) the "son of God"; the basis of imperial deity-worship.
