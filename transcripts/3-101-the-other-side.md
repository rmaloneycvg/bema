### Hebrew Words Explained

- **mamzer** — illegitimate child / outsider; the recurring "who's in, who's out" theme — here extended to the pagan Decapolis.
- **euangelion** — good news; the "gospel" of the Kingdom.
- **maim chayim** — "living water"; water that is flowing (rain or spring), as opposed to large still bodies of water that symbolize chaos.
- **Gerusim** — "the cast out ones"; a term for the seven nations driven out of Canaan (Joshua 3:10) and, in rabbinic tradition, for the Decapolis — a likely origin of the "Gerasenes/Gadarenes/Gergesenes" textual variants.
- **Legion** — the demon's name; also the Roman military unit (the 10th Legion, stationed nearby, whose standard was the boar/pig).
