### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Zadok / Zadokim** (צָדוֹק / צְדוֹקִים) — the priestly line descended from Zadok (chosen over Abiathar in David's day). *Zadokim* → "Sadducee" in English. Later it became almost a party label for any priest who agreed with the Chief Priests' Hellenized system.
- **Hasmonean Dynasty** — the century of priestly rule (handed to the Zadokim after the Maccabean revolt) that quickly became thoroughly Hellenized and corrupt.
- **Chief Priests** — the seven Hasmonean families who ran the temple as, in effect, a corrupt religious mafia (distinct from the "teachers of the law").
- **booths of Ananus** — the Mishnah's phrase for the corrupt market stalls of the house of Annas (*Ananus* in Hebrew).
- **Levi'im** (לְוִיִּם) — the Levites; all Zadokim were Levites, but not all Levites were Zadokim.
- **yeshiva** (יְשִׁיבָה) — a Jewish school; the Wohl Museum / Herodian Quarter dig sits beneath one.
- **Sanhedrin** (סַנְהֶדְרִין) — the ruling council: the formal 70-72-member body (half Pharisee, half Sadducee) and an informal, corrupt one that met in the high priest's house.
