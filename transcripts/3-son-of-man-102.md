### Hebrew Words Explained

- **ben adam** — "son of man" / literally "son of Adam"; in Daniel's vision, the figure who comes to the Ancient of Days to render justice. The sages linked "son of Adam" to Abel (the first to suffer for doing right). Jesus claims this Son of Man has authority to *forgive* rather than only to judge.
- **mamzer** — illegitimate child / outsider; Matthew's recurring agenda (tax collectors, sinners).
- **remez** — a "hint"; a deliberate quotation of Scripture meant to point the hearer back to the fuller context (e.g., Jesus quoting Hosea 6:6).
- **p'shat** — the plain, surface-level meaning of a text.
- **drash** — the deeper truth "buried" in the text; it expands the p'shat but never contradicts it.
- **mishpat** — justice.
- **tzedakah** — righteousness; generosity/justice toward others.
