### Hebrew Words Explained

- **s'micha** — authority (to interpret and teach Torah).
- **euangelion** — good news; gospel.
- **kadosh** — holy; set apart, different.
- **angaria** — the Roman legal right of a soldier to compel a civilian to carry his pack for one mile.
