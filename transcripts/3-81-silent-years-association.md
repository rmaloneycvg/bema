### Hebrew Words Explained

This reflection episode revisits the five responses to Hellenism rather than introducing new Hebrew terms. Key recurring vocabulary: the **Hasidim** (pious ones) splitting into **Zealots** and **P'rushim** (Pharisees); the **Sadducees**, **Herodians**, and **Essenes**; and the Essene motto "to know the path and to walk the path."
