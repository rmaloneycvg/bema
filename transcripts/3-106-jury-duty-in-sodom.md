### Hebrew Words Explained

- **mamzer** (also **mumzer**) — illegitimate child / outsider; Matthew's agenda. The triangle's sin is failing to care for them.
- **PaRDeS** — the four levels of Jewish interpretation: **p'shat** (plain/surface), **remez** (hint pointing to another text), **drash** (deeper derived meaning), **sod** (mystery/secret).
- **the Triangle** — Chorazin, Bethsaida, and Capernaum; the devoted, orthodox Hasidic heartland Jesus pronounces woes over (they *believed* but didn't *repent*).
- **yoke** — a rabbi's interpretation of Torah / way of life; Jesus offers one whose burden is "light" (light, not easy).
