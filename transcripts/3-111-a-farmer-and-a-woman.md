### Hebrew Words Explained

- **PaRDeS** — the four interpretive levels: **p'shat** (surface), **remez** (hint to a Hebrew text), **drash** (derived truth), **sod** (God-given mystery).
- **mustard seed** — a toxic, unstoppable weed, and the smallest of seeds; the parable's shock is calling the Kingdom something invasive that "becomes a tree" (which mustard doesn't). Both signal a *three-part eschatology*: the Kingdom starts tiny and grows, rather than crashing in all at once.
- **leaven / yeast** — normally a symbol of *sin* (removed at Passover; sacrifices must be unleavened). Calling the Kingdom "like leaven" is deliberately counterintuitive.
- **three seahs / "three measures"** of flour (~60 lbs) — the remez to Sarah baking for the three visitors (Genesis 18); radical hospitality to outsiders.
- **mamzer** (also **mumzer**) — Matthew's agenda: the Kingdom bends toward blessing the outsider.
- **birds of the air** — in Ezekiel, the Gentile nations nesting in the tree (God's people fulfilling the call to bless all nations).
