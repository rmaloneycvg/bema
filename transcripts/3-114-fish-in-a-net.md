### Hebrew Words Explained

- **PaRDeS** — p'shat (surface), remez (hint to a Hebrew text), drash (derived truth), sod (God-given mystery).
- **fish = Gentile nations** — Ezekiel's animals (birds, fish) represent the nations. Rabbi Akiva's midrash counted "153 kinds of fish" (not biology) = the 153 pagan nations identified in his day; the parable is about blessing the Gentiles, not sorting them.
- **the angels do the sorting** — as with the weeds, judgment between good/bad is God's job (at the end of the age); the disciples' job is just to carry out the mission.
- **the bad are removed, the good remain** — contra "Left Behind" rapture theology; in all these parables it's the *wicked* that are taken away.
- **furnace** — first appears at Sodom and Gomorrah (Genesis 19, failed hospitality); also Daniel's fiery furnace (perseverance) and the famous midrash of Abraham thrown into Nimrod's furnace (standing firm vs. his brother Haran hedging his bets).
- **the discipled teacher** — "every teacher of the law who has become a disciple" brings out treasures "new and old" (remez to Song of Solomon 7:13); you must be willing to un-know and relearn.
