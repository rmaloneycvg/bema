### Hebrew Words Explained

- **mitzvoth** (Hebrew, plural of *mitzvah*, “commandments / deeds”) — the episode's title and key word. When James says “works/deeds,” he means *mitzvoth* (acts of obedience), NOT the **miqsat ma'ase haTorah** (*erga nomou*, the Jewish identity-marker “works of the law”) that Paul argued Gentiles need not keep. James and Paul use different words for different audiences — so they are not contradicting each other.
- **three parts of the law** — ethical law, cultic law, and *miqsat ma'ase haTorah*. James addresses *obedience* (ethical/*mitzvoth*), not the identity markers.
- **“brother” = kinsman** — James calls himself “brother of Jesus,” but Hebraic *brother* means *kinsman*; James is likely Jesus's **cousin**, James son of Alphaeus — one of the Twelve, the James of “Peter, James, and John,” and leader of the Jerusalem church. (Same point made in Galatians; cf. Abraham calling Lot/Sarah “kinsman.”)
- **the three pillars** (Galatians 2) — Peter (over the whole movement), James (Jerusalem/Jewish church), John (pastor to Asia). Likely all three were *firstborn sons* (*bechor*), which is why Jesus took Peter, James, and John to keep **leyl shimurim** (the Passover “Night of Watching,” kept by firstborn sons, per Exodus 12).
- **tekton** (Greek, “builder”) — Joseph was a *tekton*, more likely a **stonemason** than a woodworker (wood was for the wealthy); possibly employed in Herod's quarry between Nazareth and Zippori — raising unanswerable questions about the family's leanings (Herodian? zealot, given **Cana** / *qanah*? Hasidim?).
- **“Abraham believed God and it was credited as righteousness”** (James 2:23) — James quotes the *same* verse as Paul but to the *opposite* end: Paul showed it came *before circumcision*; James shows Abraham's faith was *completed by action* (offering Isaac). Not justification by circumcision, but faith *in action*.
- **Rahab** — a Gentile prostitute cited alongside Abraham, hinting the “faith in action” truth is for Jew and Gentile alike (hence no appeal to circumcision).
- **the tongue / chiasm** — James is a literary/chiastic masterpiece; Marty's favored center is James 3 (the tongue — the *drash*: careful how you talk about the Gentiles, lest gossip/slander/favoritism undermine God's work of radical inclusion), with an alternative center at James 3:17-18 (“wisdom from heaven”). Noah/Noahic-covenant allusions run through the letter (the Jews live in “the world of Noah” among Gentiles).
