### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Gethsemane (gat shemanim)** — "oil press" in the Kidron Valley, where Jesus prays.
- **"Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani"** — "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" (opening of Psalm 22). Dying and gasping, Jesus's cry (**Eli / Eliyahu** = "Yahweh is God") is misheard by bystanders as calling for **Elijah**.
- **the sponge on hyssop** — harmonizing the Gospels, likely a Roman soldier's mockery: hyssop leaves were used as toilet paper, so the "drink" offered was a cruel jest, not relief.
- **Preparation Day / Passover timing** — Marty's reading: because Passover fell on the Sabbath that year, the meal was eaten Thursday night; the Passover *sacrifice* was Friday (Jesus as the Passover lamb); resurrection on the following Sunday = **First Fruits**. By the Jewish calendar (Friday night, Saturday, Sunday morning) that is "three days."
- **crucifixion** — reserved by Rome largely for insurgents/"terrorists" (zealots), to make a public spectacle.
- **naming** — many Jesuses, Marys, and Josephs; hence "son of [father]" mattered more than the given name.
- **atonement theories** (to be covered in Session 4): ransom/captive, Christus Victor, (penal) substitutionary, etc. — "a bag of golf clubs," each with its place.
