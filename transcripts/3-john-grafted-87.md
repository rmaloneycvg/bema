### Hebrew Words Explained

- **logos** (λόγος, Greek) — "word"; in Greek philosophy (Plato, Aristotle, Philo of Alexandria) the transcendent reality holding the universe together. John opens "In the beginning was the logos" — then shocks Greek readers by making the logos a *person* (Jesus).
- **Torah** (תּוֹרָה) — the Jewish reading of John 1: the sages held Torah/Wisdom pre-existed creation and *was* God. A Jew hears "In the beginning was Torah... and Torah was God" — then is startled that John makes Torah a person. John speaks to both audiences in the same lines: Jesus is the logos *and* Jesus is Torah.
- **grafted** (cf. Romans 11) — John's audience in Asia is a *grafted* family: Jews and an influx of Gentiles together; he needs a euangelion for both at once.
- **the seven "I Am" statements** — each confronts a pagan deity (bread of life/Demeter, light of the world/Apollo, the door/Janus, good shepherd/Pan, resurrection and life/Asclepius, way-truth-life/Athena, true vine/Dionysus) *and* claims a Jewish title of Torah simultaneously.
