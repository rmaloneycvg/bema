### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Documentary Hypothesis** (the **Wellhausen Theory**) — the widely held scholarly view that the Torah weaves together at least four distinguishable authorial voices (detectable in Hebrew word usage), compiled much later. The hosts argue this, far from undermining inspiration, highlights God's fingerprints (e.g. the Torah's chiastic structure across multiple hands).
- **redactor** — the compiler who gathers the stories/sources and weaves them into one narrative.
- **Son of Man** — Daniel's figure (Daniel 7:13-14) "coming with the clouds," given everlasting dominion; the title Jesus most often takes for himself.
- **Ancient of Days** — the enthroned figure of God before whom the Son of Man is led.
- **the abomination that causes desolation** — Daniel 9's image; read in its likely mid-2nd-century-BC setting as an indictment of the corrupt Hasmonean priesthood (not only of a distant future).
