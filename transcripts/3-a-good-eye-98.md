### Hebrew Words Explained

- **ayin tovah** — a "good eye"; seeing the world generously and optimistically, leading to a posture of abundance and generosity.
- **ayin ra'ah** — a "bad eye"; seeing the world stingily/scarcely, leading to worry and ungenerosity.
- **tzedakah** — righteousness; generosity, caring for others.
- **stringing pearls** — a rabbinic practice of connecting teachings/wisdom across Scripture; a "pearl" is a teaching.
- **dogs / pigs** — Hebrew figures of speech for Gentiles (not primarily derogatory).
- **mumzer** — illegitimate child; referenced as a comparison for how "dog" is a figure of speech, not a direct insult.
