### Hebrew Words Explained

- **talmid / talmidim** (תלמיד / תלמידים) — "disciple(s)"; Chris is introduced as a *talmid*.
- **tohu va'vohu** (תהו ובהו) — "chaos / formless void"; the biblical creation brings order out of chaos, contrasted with the Egyptian narrative.
- **yada** (ידע) — "to know" experientially and intimately (not merely cerebral); God acts in the plagues so that Israel, the Egyptians, and Pharaoh will all *yada* that he is the LORD.
- **mammon** — "wealth" (a later figure/god); Jesus' "you cannot serve both God and mammon" — you cannot serve both empire and shalom.
