### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Zealots / Kanaim** (קַנָּאִים) — one of the two Hasidim groups (with the Pharisees) who settled the Galilee; freedom fighters who bought into the "myth of redemptive violence" against Rome and the Hellenized priesthood.
- **qanah** (קָנָא) — zeal / jealousy; the root behind "I am a jealous God" — not envy but the passionate jealousy of a lover for the beloved. The Zealots took their name and spirit from it.
- **Hasidim** (חֲסִידִים) — "pious ones"; the devoted remnant who rejected Hellenistic compromise and went north to the Galilee.
- **Sicarii / sicar** (Latin) — a sect of Zealots named for the *sicar*, the dagger they hid up their sleeves to assassinate Romans.
- **havurah** (חֲבוּרָה) — the fellowship/community; "there is a place in the havurah" for the zealous.
