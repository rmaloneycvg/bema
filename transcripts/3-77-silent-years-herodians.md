### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Herodian** — a (loosely defined) party or disposition named for Herod the Great: non-priestly Jews who, like the Sadducees, embraced Hellenism — believing they could keep both their worship of God and the comfort, luxury, art, and entertainment of Rome. The hosts argue most modern American Christians are, at heart, Herodian.
- **tekton** (τέκτων, Greek) — "craftsman/builder," the word for Joseph's trade (usually rendered "carpenter"); in that region more likely a worker in *stone* than wood.
- **hupokrites** (ὑποκριτής, Greek) — "actor" (from the theater); the root of "hypocrite," a word Jesus uses — a hint of exposure to theater.
- **triclinium** (Latin) — the three-sided reclining dining arrangement around a central mosaic — the "dinner and a show" centerpiece of a wealthy villa.
- **Cardo / Decumanus** (Latin) — the two main cross-streets (north-south and east-west) around which Greco-Roman cities were gridded.
