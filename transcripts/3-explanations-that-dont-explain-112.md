### Hebrew Words Explained

- **PaRDeS** — p'shat (surface), remez (hint to a Hebrew text), drash (derived truth), sod (God-given mystery). Jesus's "explanation" of the weeds doesn't actually explain — it clarifies the variables while adding *more* remez and drash.
- **shibbolet / shabbolet** — "ear of grain" (also the famous Judges 12 password); here the "bastard wheat" (tares) that is indistinguishable from wheat until harvest, when real wheat bows and the tares stand up straight.
- **three-part eschatology** — this parable most clearly affirms it: weeds and wheat grow *together* until a future harvest, rather than a single crashing judgment (two-part).
- From the Orthodox Jewish Bible rendering: **Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach** (Jesus the Messiah King), **beit** / **beit av** (house/household), **talmidim** (disciples), **mashal** (parable), **zera tov** (good seed), **Ben haAdam** (Son of Man), **HaOlam Hazeh** (this world/age), **B'nei HaMalchut** (children of the Kingdom), **b'nei haRah** (children of the evil one), **HaSatan** (the accuser/adversary), **katzir / katzirim** (harvest/harvesters), **melochim** (angels/messengers).
- **ayin tovah / ayin ra'ah** — good eye / bad eye (noted in the Hebrew word play).
- Dancing at a passage you don't understand — the Jewish posture of delight that a future "aha" awaits, versus Western frustration.
