### Hebrew Words Explained

- **mamzer** — illegitimate child; used poetically for the outsiders/outcasts Matthew's Gospel keeps bringing in (lepers, a centurion, Gentiles from "the East and the West").
- **tzara'at** — the Hebrew term for "leprosy" (a range of skin conditions, including things like severe acne under Levitical code).
- **eremos topos** — a desolate/solitary place; the "wilderness" location away from the crowds.
- **chutzpah** — bold audacity; the nerve shown by those (like the leper or the bleeding woman) who push past social boundaries to reach Jesus.
- **Beth'ani / Bethany** — possibly "house of misery," theorized to be a leper colony where Lazarus lived.
- **ossuary** — the bone box used after a body decomposed, placed in the family tomb on the one-year anniversary of death.
