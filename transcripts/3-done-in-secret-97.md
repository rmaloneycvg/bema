**1** “Be careful not to display your righteousness merely to be seen by people. ### Hebrew Words Explained

- **hupocritos** (Greek) — "actor"; the word translated "hypocrite." For Jesus it means doing righteousness as a show, not two-faced behavior.
- **Amidah** — the standing prayer prayed daily; Jesus's "Lord's Prayer" follows its format and sections, adding the line about forgiving our debtors.
- **kadosh** — holy; set apart (echoed in "hallowed/holy be your name").
