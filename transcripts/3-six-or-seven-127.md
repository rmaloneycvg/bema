### Hebrew Words Explained

- **Moses' seat** — the synagogue chair where the one reading the Text sits. "Do what they say" = do what the *Text* says (they faithfully read/teach it); "don't do what they do" = don't follow their **halakhah** (their *walk*/interpretation).
- **halakhah** — how one walks out the Text. Jesus affirms the Pharisees' teaching and devotion but critiques their interpretation-in-practice (and how they load burdens on people).
- **hupocritos** — "actor"; a hypocrite is one who performs righteousness for show — you can do *exactly* what you say and still be a hypocrite, because it's for an audience.
- **phylacteries / tassels** — worn conspicuously wide/long for display.
- **Rabbi / Father / Instructor** — titles not to be sought for status; it's about servanthood, not position.
- **six woes vs. seven woes** — Isaiah 5 has *six* woes describing a person of **greed** (6 = sinful man). Jesus gives *seven* woes describing a person of **self-righteousness** (7 = completion). By adding the seventh, Jesus says they have *completed/perfected* the art of sin — "go ahead then and complete what your ancestors started."
- **justice, mercy, faithfulness** — the weightier matters neglected while tithing mint, dill, and cumin.
- Jesus's point: self-righteousness is worse than greed, because it puts a counterfeit God on display.
